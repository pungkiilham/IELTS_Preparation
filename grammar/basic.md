# Basic English Review

## Overview
This document covers foundational English grammar concepts that frequently cause problems for IELTS learners. Use this alongside grammar_guide.md.

---

## 1.Sentence Types

### Simple Sentences
One subject + one verb = complete idea.

| Example |
|---------|
| The student studies. |
| Birds fly. |
| She works. |

### Compound Sentences
Two simple sentences joined by a conjunction.

| Conjunction | Example |
|-------------|---------|
| and | I like tea and she likes coffee. |
| but | I am tired but I can't sleep. |
| or | Study hard or you will fail. |
| so | It was raining so we stayed home. |
| because | I stayed home because I was sick. |

### Complex Sentences
Main clause + dependent clause.

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| Because clause | Because it rained, we stayed home. |
| If clause | If you work hard, you will succeed. |
| When clause | When I arrived, the movie started. |
| Although clause | Although it was hard, I passed. |

## 2. Question Forms

### Yes/No Questions
| Statement | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| You are a student. | Are you a student? |
| She works here. | Does she work here? |
| They finished. | Did they finish? |

### Wh- Questions
| Question Word | Use | Example |
|---------------|-----|---------|
| What | Things | What is your name? |
| Who | People | Who called? |
| When | Time | When did you arrive? |
| Where | Place | Where do you live? |
| Why | Reason | Why are you late? |
| How | Manner | How did you come? |
| Which | Choice | Which one do you want? |
| Whose | Possession | Whose book is this? |

### Question Tags
| Statement | Tag | Meaning |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| It's raining, isn't it? | isn't it | confirming |
| You're a student, aren't you? | aren't you | confirming |
| She can't come, can she? | can she | surprising |
| He doesn't know, does he? | does he | uncertain |

## 3. Countable vs Uncountable Nouns

### Countable Nouns
Can count: one book, two books, three books

| Countable | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| a book | I have a book. |
| two books | I have two books. |
| many books | I have many books. |
| a few books | I have a few books. |

### Uncountable Nouns
Cannot count: water, information, advice

| Uncountable | Example |
|------------|---------|
| water | I need water. |
| some water | I need some water. |
| much water | We don't need much water. |
| a little water | Give me a little water. |

### Countable and Uncountable Patterns

| Pattern | Countable | Uncountable |
|---------|----------|------------|
| Positive | I have books. | I have information. |
| Negative | I don't have books. | I don't have information. |
| Question | Do you have books? | Do you have information? |
| a/an | a book | - |
| some | some books | some water |
| any | any books | any water |
| many | many books | much water |
| few/a few | few books | a little water |
| every | every book | - |

### Common Uncountable Nouns

| Category | Words |
|----------|-------|
| Abstract | advice, information, news, progress, research |
| Liquids | water, milk, oil, tea, coffee |
| Materials | furniture, luggage, clothing, jewelry |
| Activities | work, homework, research |
| Feelings | happiness, honesty, courage |
| Languages | English, Chinese, French |
| Fields of Study | history, physics, mathematics |

###Making Uncountable Countable
| Uncountable | Countable Version |
|-------------|-------------------|
| an advice | a piece of advice |
| a furniture | a piece of furniture |
| a information | a piece of information |
| a news | a piece of news / a news item |

## 4. Pronouns

### Subject Pronouns vs Object Pronouns

| Subject | Object |
|---------|--------|
| I | me |
| you | you |
| he | him |
| she | her |
| it | it |
| we | us |
| they | them |

### Possessive Adjectives

| Subject | Possessive |
|---------|-----------|
| I | my |
| you | your |
| he | his |
| she | her |
| it | its |
| we | our |
| they | their |

### Possessive Pronouns

| Possessive Adjective | Possessive Pronoun |
|-------------------|------------------|
| my book | mine |
| your book | yours |
| his book | his |
| her book | hers |
| its color | its (rarely used) |
| our book | ours |
| their book | theirs |

### Demonstratives

| This/These | That/Those |
|------------|-------------|
| this (close) | that (far) |
| these (close, plural) | those (far, plural) |
| I like this. | I like that. |
| these books | those books |

## 5. Adjectives

### Adjective Order

Use this order: Opinion - Size - Age - Shape - Color - Origin - Material - Purpose

| Order | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Opinion | beautiful |
| Size | large |
| Age | old |
| Shape | round |
| Color | red |
| Origin | French |
| Material | wooden |
| Purpose | cooking |

**Full:** A beautiful large old round red French wooden cooking pot.

### Comparatives

| Form | Use | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| -er | One-syllable adjectives | taller, faster |
| More | Two+ syllable adjectives | more beautiful |
| As...as | Equality | as tall as me |
| Less | Inequality | less interesting |

### Superlatives

| Form | Use | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| -est | One-syllable adjectives | the tallest |
| Most | Two+ syllable adjectives | the most beautiful |

### Irregular Comparatives/Superlatives

| Adjective | Comparative | Superlative |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| good | better | best |
| bad | worse | worst |
| far | farther/further | farthest/furthest |
| many/much | more | most |
| little | less | least |

### Adjective + Preposition

| Adjective | Preposition | Example |
|-----------|--------------|---------|
| good | at | good at math |
| bad | at | bad at sports |
| interested | in | interested in art |
| tired | of | tired of waiting |
| afraid | of | afraid of heights |
| full | of | full of energy |
| similar | to | similar to mine |
| different | from | different from yours |

## 6. Adverbs

### Adjective to Adverb

| Rule | Adjective | Adverb |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Most words add -ly | quick | quickly |
| y → i + ly | easy | easily |
| le → y | comfortable | comfortably |
| Exceptions: early, friendly, lovely, lonely, ugly | - | - |

### Adverb Position

| Position | Example |
|----------|---------|
| Before verb | She always arrives early. |
| After verb be | He is always happy. |
| Before adjective | She's very talented. |
| Before other adverb | She speaks quite fluently. |
| End of sentence | I study every day. |

### Common Adverbs

| Category | Words |
|----------|-------|
| Frequency | always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never |
| Time | now, then, today, yesterday, tomorrow, soon |
| Manner | quickly, slowly, carefully, easily, neatly |
| Degree | very, quite, too, quite, extremely |

## 9. Verb Patterns

### Verb + -ING

| Pattern | Verbs |
|---------|-------|
| Verb + -ING | enjoy, finish, mind, practice, suggest |
| Examples | I enjoy reading. She finished writing. |

### Verb + TO + Verb

| Pattern | Verbs |
|---------|-------|
| Verb + to + verb | want, hope, decide, plan, need, would like |
| Examples | I want to learn. She needs to go. |

### Verb + Object + TO + Verb

| Pattern | Verbs |
|---------|-------|
| Verb + object + to + verb | ask, tell, want, would like |
| Examples | I told him to wait. She asked me to help. |

### Verb + -ING or TO + Verb

| Pattern | Verbs |
|---------|-------|
| Like + -ING / to + verb | I like swimming. / I like to swim. |
| Love + -ING / to + verb | I love reading. / I love to read. |
| Hate + -ING / to + verb | I hate waiting. / I hate to wait. |
| Start/Begin + -ING or to + verb | Both correct |

### Verb + -ING (not to + verb)

| Verbs | Example |
|------|---------|
| enjoy | I enjoy reading. |
| finish | I finished reading. |
| mind | Do you mind waiting? |
| practice | I practice speaking. |
| suggest | I suggest waiting. |
| keep | Keep trying. |
| stop | Stop talking. |
| consider | Consider changing. |

### Verb + TO + Verb (not -ING)

| Verbs | Example |
|------|---------|
| want | I want to learn. |
| hope | I hope to pass. |
| need | I need to study. |
| decide | I decided to go. |
| plan | I plan to travel. |
| promise | I promise to help. |
| refuse | I refuse to answer. |

## 10. Modals

### Common Modals

| Modal | Use | Example |
|-------|-----|---------|
| can | Ability, permission | I can swim. |
| could | Past ability, polite | Could you help me? |
| may | Possibility, permission | May I come in? |
| might | Small possibility | It might rain. |
| must | Strong necessity | You must be here on time. |
| have to | Necessity (external) | I have to work. |
| should | Advice | You should study. |
| would | Past habit, polite | Would you like tea? |
| will | Future, willingness | I will help. |

### Modals for Probability

| Modal | Certainty | Example |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| must | Very certain | She must be tired. |
| might | Possible | She might be busy. |
| can't |Impossible | She can't be dead. |

### Modals for Advice

| Modal | Example |
|-------|---------|
| should | You should see a doctor. |
| ought to | You ought to apologize. |
| had better | You had better study. |

## 11. Common Mistakes

### Confusing Words

| Mistake | Correct |
|---------|---------|
| affect (verb) | effect (noun) |
| then (time) | than (comparison) |
| quiet (silent) | quite (very) |
| lose (verb) | loose (adjective) |
| accept (verb) | except (preposition) |
| passed (verb) | past (time) |

### Common Errors

| Mistake | Correct |
|---------|---------|
| I am agree. | I agree. / I am agreed. |
| I am boring. | I am bored. |
| interesting film | an interesting film |
| She is very intelligent. | She is very intelligent. OR extremely intelligent |
| I am agree with you. | I agree with you. |
| My English is not good. | My English is not good. |
| I cannot understand. | I cannot understand. OR I don't understand. |
| There is many reasons. | There are many reasons. |
| I went to market. | I went to the market. |

### Word Order Mistakes

| Mistake | Correct |
|---------|---------|
| I have very much homework. | I have a lot of homework. |
| I never have seen that. | I have never seen that. |
| I study English since 2020. | I have been studying English since 2020. |
| I speak English good. | I speak English well. |

## 12. Homophones and Confusing Words

### Common Homophones

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| their / there / they're | possessive / place / they are |
| your / you're | possessive / you are |
| its / it's | possessive / it is |
| to / too / two | preposition / also / number |
| affect / effect | verb / noun |
| principle / principal | rule / head |

### Easily Confused Words

| Word | Use | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| look | Use eyes | Look at the board. |
| watch | Observe | Watch the movie. |
| see | Visual | I see a bird. |
| hear | Sound | I hear music. |
| listen | Active hearing | Listen to music. |
| say | Words | Say hello. |
| tell | Information | Tell me a story. |
| speak | Language | Speak English. |
| talk | Conversation | Talk to your friend. |
