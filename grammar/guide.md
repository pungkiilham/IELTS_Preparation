# IELTS Grammar Guide

## Overview
This guide covers essential grammar topics for IELTS Band 6.5. Each section includes rules, examples, and common mistakes.

---

## 1. Tenses Overview

### Present Simple
**Rule:** Use for habits, general truths, facts, and scheduled events.

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| Positive | She works in London. |
| Negative | He doesn't drink coffee. |
| Question | Does the sun rise in the east? |

### Present Continuous
**Rule:** Use for actions happening now, temporary situations, and changing situations.

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| Positive | I am studying English. |
| Negative | They are not coming today. |
| Question | Is anyone listening? |

### Present Perfect
**Rule:** Use for actions that happened at an unspecified time (important for IELTS!).

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| Positive | I have visited Paris. |
| Negative | She hasn't finished her work. |
| Question | Have you ever eaten sushi? |

### Present Perfect Continuous
**Rule:** Use for actions that started in the past and continue to now, emphasizing duration.

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| Positive | I have been waiting for 2 hours. |
| Negative | He hasn't been working here long. |

### Past Simple
**Rule:** Use for completed actions in the past (specific time mentioned).

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| Positive | I called her yesterday. |
| Negative | They didn't see the movie. |

### Past Continuous
**Rule:** Use for actions in progress at a specific past time.

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| Positive | I was sleeping when she called. |
| Negative | We weren't listening. |

### Past Perfect
**Rule:** Use for actions completed before another past action.

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| Positive | By the time I arrived, she had left. |
| Negative | The movie hadn't started when we got there. |

### Future Forms
| Form | Use | Example |
|------|-----|---------|
| will + verb | Predictions, promises | It will rain tomorrow. |
| going to + verb | Plans, intentions | I am going to study medicine. |
| present continuous | Scheduled events | The train leaves at 6pm. |
| present simple | Timetables | The flight arrives at 8am. |

## 2. Subject-Verb Agreement

### Basic Rule
The subject and verb must match in number.

| Subject | Verb |
|---------|------|
| He works | works |
| They work | work |

### Collective Nouns (British English)
| Noun | Verb | Example |
|-----|------|--------|
| team | is/are | The team is playing well. |
| police | are | The police are investigating. |
| government | is/are | The government has announced... |

### Tricky Cases

#### "Data"
"The data shows" OR "The data show" - Both are correct. "Data" is Latin plural, but in modern English often treated as singular.

#### "Neither...nor" / "Either...or"
The verb agrees with the **nearest** subject.
- Neither the manager nor the employees **are** happy.
- Neither the employees nor the manager **is** happy.

#### "Each"
Always singular: "Each student **has** a book."

#### "Every"
Always singular: "Every person **knows** the answer."

#### "None"
Can be singular or plural depending on meaning:
- None of the students **is** ready. (not one)
- None of the students **are** ready. (not any)

## 3. Articles: A, An, The

### A vs An
| Use | Example |
|-----|---------|
| a + consonant sound | a book, a university, a European |
| an + vowel sound | an apple, an hour, an honest man |

**Note:** Use the **sound**, not the letter!
- a uniform (yoo = consonant)
- an honor (o = vowel)

### The
Use "the" when:
- You know the specific thing: **the** book on the table
- There's only one: **the** sun, **the** moon, **the** president
- You've mentioned it before: I bought a book. **The** book is interesting.
- With superlatives: **the** best, **the** most beautiful
- Generals: **the** rich, **the** poor (as groups)

### No Article
Use **no article** (zero article) when:
- General plural countable: Dogs are loyal.
- General uncountable: Water is essential.
- Abstract nouns: Happiness is important.
- Languages: I speak English.
- Subjects: History is interesting.
- Places (most): Go to school, go to bed, go to work

### Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correct |
|---------|---------|
| I went to university. | I went to university. (no article) |
| The information is useful. | The information is useful. (or "is" - uncountable) |
| He gave me an advice. | He gave me some advice. (advice = uncountable) |

## 4. Word Forms

### Why This Matters for IELTS
Using correct word forms improves your Lexical Resource score. This is crucial for Band 6.5+.

### Common Transformations

| Noun | Verb | Adjective | Adverb |
|-----|------|----------|-------|
| increase | increase | increasing/increased | increasingly |
| decide | decide | decisive | decisively |
| success | succeed | successful | successfully |
| dependent | depend | dependent | dependently |
| relation | relate | related | - |
| develop | develop | developed/developing | - |
| environment | - | environmental | - |
| significant | - | significant | significantly |
| differ | differ | different | differently |

### Practice: Complete the Sentence

1. The company announced a significant _____ in profits. (increase)
2. Her presentation was _____ received. (favor)
3. There is a strong _____ between smoking and cancer. (relate)
4. Students should develop _____ skills. (dependent)
5. The government took _____ action. (decide)

**Answers:** 1. increase 2. favorably 3. relationship 4. independent 5. decisive

## 5. Prepositions

### Common Preposition Patterns

#### Dependent On / Depend On
- This depends **on** funding.
- I am dependent **on** my phone.

#### Interested In / Interest In
- She is interested **in** learning.
- He has no interest **in** politics.

#### Focus On
- We need to focus **on** the main issue.
- The report focuses **on** climate change.

#### Consists Of
- The team consists **of** five members.

#### Consists In
- Happiness consists **in** being grateful.

#### Benefit From
- Students benefit **from** practice.

#### Benefit To/For
- There are no benefits **to** smoking.
- The benefit **for** companies is clear.

#### Consume/Consumption Of
- High consumption **of** sugar is bad.
- The country consumes a lot of oil.

#### Lack Of
- There is a lack **of** funding.

#### Level Of
- The level **of** education is rising.

### Prepositions After Adjectives

| Adjective | Preposition | Example |
|----------|------------|---------|
| interested | in | interested in art |
| dependent | on | dependent on parents |
| good | at | good at math |
| different | from | different from mine |
| tired | of | tired of waiting |
| afraid | of | afraid of heights |
| sure | about | sure about it |

## 6. Conditionals

### Zero Conditional
**Real conditions, general truths**

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| If + present simple, present simple | If you heat ice, it melts. |
| Present simple + if | Water freezes if temperature drops below 0. |

### First Conditional
**Real possibilities in the future**

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| If + present simple, will + verb | If it rains, I will stay home. |

### Second Conditional
**Hypothetical/imaginary situations**

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| If + past simple, would + verb | If I won the lottery, I would travel. |

### Third Conditional
**Past hypothetical - things that didn't happen**

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| If + past perfect, would have + past participle | If I had studied, I would have passed. |

### Mixed Conditional
**Past condition, present result**

| Form | Example |
|------|---------|
| If + past perfect, would + verb | If I had worked harder, I would be successful now. |

### Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Correct |
|---------|---------|
| If I will come... | If I come... |
| I would come if I would be invited. | I would come if I were invited. (Second conditional - use "were") |
| If I would have known... | If I had known... |

### Unless = If Not
- You will fail unless you study. = You will fail if you don't study.

## 7. Passive Voice

### When to Use Passive
- When the agent is unknown: The window was broken.
- When the agent is unimportant: Paper is made from wood.
- In formal writing: It is believed that... / It is considered that...
- For IELTS Task 1: The chart shows that... / The figure indicates that...

### Passive Forms

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|------|--------|---------|
| Simple Present | They build houses. | Houses are built. |
| Present Perfect | They have built a house. | A house has been built. |
| Past Simple | They built a hospital. | A hospital was built. |
| Past Perfect | They had finished. | It had been finished. |
| Future | They will announce results. | Results will be announced. |
| Modal | They must finish. | It must be finished. |

### By + Agent
- The house was built **by** workers.
- The letter was written **by** my brother.

### With/Without Object
- She was given a book. (two objects)
- A book was given to her. / She was given a book.

### Formal Passive (for Writing)
| Active | Passive |
|--------|---------|
| People believe that... | It is believed that... |
| They think that... | It is thought that... |
| They say that... | It is said that... |
| We know that... | It is known that... |
| You should remember... | It should be remembered... |

## 8. Relative Clauses

### Defining vs Non-Defining

#### Defining (no commas needed)
- The **man who** works here is my brother.
- The **book that** I bought is interesting.

#### Non-Defining (commas needed)
- My brother**, who** works here, is tall.
- The book**, which** I bought yesterday**,** is interesting.

### Relative Pronouns

| Purpose | People | Things |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Subject | who/that | which/that |
| Object | who/that | which/that |
| Possessive | whose | whose/of which |

### Whose
- The **man whose** car was stolen called the police.
- The **company whose** profits increased hired more staff.

### Combining Sentences with Relative Clauses

**Original:** The man works at the bank. His father is a doctor.
**Combined:** The man **whose** father is a doctor works at the bank.

**Original:** I bought the book yesterday. It is on the table.
**Combined:** The book **that/which** I bought yesterday is on the table.

## 9. Complex Sentences

### -ING and -ED Clauses

#### -ING (active, ongoing)
- The **report revealing** surprising findings was published.
- **Working** hard is important.

#### -ED (passive, completed)
- The **weather having improved**, we went hiking.
- **Given** more time, I would have finished.

### Linking Words

#### Addition
- Furthermore, Moreover, Additionally, In addition

#### Contrast
- However, Nevertheless, Although, Despite

#### Cause/Result
- Therefore, Consequently, As a result, Thus

#### Examples
- For instance, For example, Such as

### Common Sentence Combinations

**Using -ING:**
The report was published, **revealing** surprising findings.

**Using -ED:**
The weather improved. We went hiking.
→ **Having improved**, the weather allowed us to go hiking.

**Using because:**
He failed. He didn't study.
→ He failed **because** he didn't study.

**Using so:**
She was exhausted. She couldn't continue.
→ She was exhausted, **so** she couldn't continue.

**Using although:**
Although it was raining, we went out.
→ **Despite** the rain, we went out.

## 10. Common Grammar Mistakes for IELTS

### 1. Double Negatives
| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| I don't know nothing. | I don't know anything. |
| She hasn't got no money. | She hasn't got any money. |

### 2. Its vs It's
| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| Its a good idea. | It's a good idea. |
| The dog lost its collar. | (possessive = its) |

### 3. Subject-Verb with "Data"
| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| The data shows... | The data show / shows... |
| The population are increasing. | The population is increasing. |

### 4. Double Conjunctions
| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| Because it was rainy, so we stayed. | Because it was rainy, we stayed. / It was rainy, so we stayed. |
| Although she was tired, but she worked. | Although she was tired, she worked. |

### 5. Countable vs Uncountable
| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| an advice | a piece of advice / some advice |
| furnitures | furniture |
| informations | information |

### 6. Articles with General Concepts
| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| The honesty is important. | Honesty is important. |
| The water is precious. | Water is precious. |

### 7. Too vs Very
| Use | Example |
|-----|---------|
| Too + adjective/adverb (excessive) | It's too hot to work. |
| Very + adjective/adverb (intensive) | It's very hot today. |

### 8. Make vs Do
| Make | Do |
|------|-----|
| make a decision | do homework |
| make a profit | do business |
| make noise | do a test |
| make an effort | do work |

## 11. IELTS Writing Task 1 Grammar

### Describing Trends

| Trend | Verbs | Examples |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Upward | increase, rise, grow, climb | increased significantly |
| Downward | decrease, fall, decline, drop | fell dramatically |
| Stable | remain steady, stay constant | remained stable |
| Peak | reach a peak, peak at | peaked at 50% |
| Low | reach a low, bottom out | bottomed out at 10% |

### Time Expressions
- Over the period
- During the period
- From 1990 to 2000
- Between 1990 and 2000

### Passive in Task 1
- The figure indicates that...
- The chart shows that...
- It can be seen that...
- Sales were made in...
- Energy was consumed by...

## 12. IELTS Speaking Grammar

### Avoiding Basic Mistakes

#### Article "The" with Uniques
| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| I like the sun. | I like sun and sand. |

#### Uncountable Nouns
| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| I have much work. | I have a lot of work. |
| She gave me an advice. | She gave me some advice. |

#### Countable Nouns
| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| I need a advice. | I need some advice. |
| There is many reason. | There are many reasons. |

### Advanced Structures for Band 6.5+

#### Modal Verbs for Hedging
- It **might** be true.
- It **could** suggest that...
- This **may** indicate...

#### Complex Sentences
- Having finished my studies, I started working.
- If I had more money, I would travel more.

#### Conditionals in Speaking
- **If** I were you, I'd...
- I'd rather **than**...
- What **if** something goes wrong?

### Fluency Markers (Use Naturally)
- Well, let me think...
- Actually, that's an interesting question...
- You know what I mean?
- Basically, the thing is...
