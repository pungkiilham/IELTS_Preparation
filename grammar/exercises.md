## Exercise 5: Conditionals
18. worked (Second Conditional - hypothetical)
19. would have been (Third Conditional - mixed)
20. melts (Zero Conditional - general truth)

## Exercise 6: Passive Voice

23. A new species has been discovered.
24. The results will be announced tomorrow.

## Exercise 7: Relative Clauses

## Exercise 8: Error Correction
27. I don't know anything about this topic.
28. It's a good idea to start early.
29. The data show that the population is increasing.
30. Because the weather was bad, the flight was delayed. OR The weather was bad, so the flight was delayed.

## Exercise 9: Sentence Transformation

32. Despite studying very hard, he failed the exam.
33. They may arrive late.
34. The government increased taxes; consequently, consumer spending decreased.
35. Unless we protect the environment, it will be destroyed.

## Exercise 10: Complex Structures

38. He failed the exam because he didn't study.
39. She was exhausted, so she couldn't continue working.
