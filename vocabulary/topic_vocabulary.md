## Daily Practice Routine

1. **Morning**: Read 1 topic (10 words)
2. **Before writing**: Check vocabulary list for topic synonyms
3. **After writing**: Add new words to personal vocabulary list

## Tips for Usage

- **Use words in context** - Don't memorize alone; use in sentences
- **Learn collocations** - Words that commonly go together
- **Replace basic words** - Swap "big" → "substantial", "many" → "numerous"
- **Practice with real essays** - Apply vocabulary in your writing practice
