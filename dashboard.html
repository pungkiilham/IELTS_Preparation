📝 Writing Tips for Band 7

1. Vocabulary

Use specific names: "FAANG companies" instead of "big companies". Add numbers: "$150,000+" instead of "high salary".

2. Examples

Replace vague examples with data-driven ones. Use: names, statistics, years, specific companies.

3. Linking Words

Upgrade: "However" → "Nevertheless", "Also" → "Furthermore", "So" → "Therefore"

4. Grammar

Use complex sentences: "Students who develop specialized expertise... enjoy higher rates"

5. No Personal Anecdotes

IELTS Writing is formal. Remove "my case", "I experienced". Use universal examples instead.

6. Opinion Justification

State your position clearly + explain WHY with 2-3 supporting sentences.
