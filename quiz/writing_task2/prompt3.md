## Example Essays

### Band 6.0 Example (~260 words)

It is undeniable that the number of crime cases is increasing in many countries around the world. This essay will discuss the main causes of this phenomenon and suggest solutions to reduce crime rates.

The primary cause of rising crime is unemployment and economic inequality. When people cannot find stable jobs, they may turn to illegal activities to support themselves and their families. For example, in developing countries, many young adults who graduate from university cannot find suitable employment, leading some to resort to theft or fraud. Furthermore, the gap between the rich and poor continues to widen, creating resentment and desperation among those who feel left behind.

To address these issues, governments should invest in education and job training programs. By providing vocational training and skills development, unemployed individuals can gain the qualifications needed to secure well-paying jobs. Additionally, governments can create more employment opportunities by attracting foreign investment and supporting small businesses. When people have stable incomes, they are less likely to engage in criminal activities.

In conclusion, the increase in crime is largely driven by unemployment and economic inequality. By investing in education and creating more job opportunities, governments can effectively reduce crime rates and create a more stable society.



### Band 9.0 Example (~290 words)

It is widely acknowledged that many nations are experiencing a significant increase in criminal activity. This essay will examine the principal causes of this troubling trend and propose viable solutions to mitigate it.

One of the primary drivers of rising crime rates is socioeconomic inequality coupled with limited access to education. When individuals, particularly in disadvantaged communities, lack the necessary skills and qualifications to secure decent employment, they may resort to illegal means of generating income. Moreover, the widening gap between the wealthy and the poor fosters a sense of injustice and desperation, which can culminate in criminal behaviour. Research consistently demonstrates that areas with high unemployment and inadequate educational facilities tend to exhibit elevated crime statistics.

To combat this issue, governments must prioritise investment in education and vocational training programmes. By equipping young people with marketable skills, societies can create a more employable workforce capable of contributing meaningfully to the economy. Additionally, implementing robust social welfare systems can provide a safety net for vulnerable populations, thereby reducing the temptation to engage in unlawful activities. Community-based policing and rehabilitation initiatives have also proven effective in addressing the root causes of criminal behaviour.

In conclusion, while socioeconomic factors and educational limitations undoubtedly contribute to escalating crime rates, these challenges can be addressed through comprehensive governmental strategies. By prioritising education, creating employment opportunities, and strengthening social support systems, societies can effectively curb criminal activity and foster safer, more inclusive communities.

## example from internet
Example IELTS Problem-Solution Essay
Topic: In many countries, the amount of crime is increasing. What are the causes of this, and what are some possible solutions?


Introduction
It is true that many nations are currently facing a surge in crime rates, leading to increased public insecurity. This essay will discuss how rising poverty and lack of educational opportunities contribute to this issue, and suggest that government investment in education and community programs are effective solutions. 


Body Paragraph 1: Causes
The primary cause of the rising crime rate is the widening gap between the rich and the poor, compounded by a lack of educational opportunities. When individuals, particularly the youth, cannot secure well-paying jobs due to poor education or a lack of skills, they may turn to criminal activities to survive. Furthermore, rapid urbanization often leaves many people living in slums with limited access to social services, which creates an environment where petty theft and violent crime can thrive. 


Body Paragraph 2: Solutions
To address these issues, governments must prioritize investment in education and create job opportunities. Specifically, implementing free, quality vocational training programs can empower underprivileged youths to gain relevant skills, thereby reducing the need for illegal activities. Additionally, local governments can improve community safety by investing in youth centers and enhancing social support services, which can provide a safer environment and reduce temptation to engage in criminal behavior.


Conclusion
In conclusion, the increase in crime is largely driven by poverty and limited opportunities, but these issues can be mitigated. By investing in education and social infrastructure, governments can tackle the root causes of crime, ensuring a more stable and secure society.

Key Tips for Success
Structure: Introduction -> Problems -> Solutions -> Conclusion.
Balance: Spend equal time on causes and solutions.
Connect Solutions to Causes: If you say lack of education is the cause, education-focused solutions must follow.
Keep it Realistic: Offer practical solutions (e.g., "improve public transport") rather than impossible ones (e.g., "ban all cars").
Drafting: Spend 5 minutes planning, 30 minutes writing, and 5 minutes editing.
