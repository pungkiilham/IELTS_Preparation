## Speaking Tips

### Useful Phrases
- "One skill I learned..."
- "My [mother/father/teacher] taught me..."
- "I remember clearly how..."
- "This skill has been really useful because..."

### Structure (PEARL Method)
- **P**oint: What skill and who taught you
- **E**xplain: How you learned it
- **A**ctual Example: A specific memory
- **R**eason: Why it's useful
- **L**ink: Connect to your life today

### Time Tracker
- 0:00-0:30 (Introduction): What skill + who taught you
- 0:30-1:00 (Body): How you learned it (steps)
- 1:00-1:30 (Conclusion): Why it's useful today
