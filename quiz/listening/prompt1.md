## Script: A Conversation at a Hotel

**Part 1: Questions 1-5**

Listen to the conversation between a hotel receptionist and a guest. Complete the notes below.

**Write NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER for each answer.**

---

**RECEPTIONIST:** Good afternoon, Sunshine Hotel. How can I help you?

**GUEST:** Hello, I'd like to book a room for three nights, please. Starting from the 15th of June.

**RECEPTIONIST:**Certainly, sir. What type of room would you prefer? We have single rooms at $80 per night, double rooms at $120, and suites at $200.

**GUEST:** I'll take a double room, please. Is breakfast included?

**RECEPTIONIST:** Yes, it is. And we offer free WiFi throughout the hotel. Could I have your name, please?

**GUEST:** It's Michael Chen. That's C-H-E-N.

**RECEPTIONIST:** Thank you, Mr Chen. And may I ask for your credit card details to confirm the booking?

**GUEST:** Sure. It's 4521 6789 0123 4567.

**RECEPTIONIST:** Perfect. The total comes to $360, which will be charged to your card. Check-in time is 2 PM. Will you be arriving before 10 PM?

**GUEST:** Yes, probably around 7 PM.

**RECEPTIONIST:** Great! If you need to cancel, please let us know 24 hours in advance. Is there anything else?

**GUEST:** Yes, do you have parking facilities?

**RECEPTIONIST:** Yes, we do. Parking is $15 per day, or $80 per week. Would you like to include that?

**GUEST:** Yes, please. I'll need it for all three days.

**RECEPTIONIST:** That adds another $45. So your total is $405. Thank you, Mr Chen. We look forward to seeing you on the 15th.

**GUEST:** Thank you. Goodbye.
